The challenge

Sustainability & national libraries

National libraries safeguard a nation's published heritage, preserving it for future generations. We collect for legal, scholarly, and cultural reasons, though the nature of published content is changing. Our collections are increasingly hybrid: stored not only on shelves but also on servers. ReVerDi asks what an optimal mix of physical and digital looks like, and measures it across three dimensions: environmental, economic, and social. Is print or digital better, for whom, and in what circumstances? How can we truly quantify the impact of collecting?

E

Environmental

Energy, CO₂, water and resource use across the full life cycle of physical and digital media.

€

Economic

Investment and operating costs — and the public value libraries create for society.

S

Social & cultural

Access, inclusion, working conditions and the safeguarding of cultural heritage.
