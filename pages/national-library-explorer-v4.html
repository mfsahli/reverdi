The Newspaper Game

You want to read your favourite newspaper. At the British Library that is The Daily Mirror, at the KB De Telegraaf, at the Swiss National Library the Basler Zeitung. Between you and your paper lie three decisions — let's walk through them together.

You play Folio. Where the road forks, the road you take is your answer; where a wall of doors blocks the way, step through the one you choose. The journey ends at a door — the reading room if you chose paper, the library's digital space if you chose the screen — and behind it, the footprint of that single reading.

🏅 Bonus: papers 📗 and discs 💿 lie along the road — some in tricky spots. Gather every single one and Folio earns the Golden Library Card.
